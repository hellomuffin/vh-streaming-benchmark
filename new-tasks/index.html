Tasks v3 — one goal, a house left wrong

Each task is a single natural goal — put things back where they belong, load all the dishes, put the groceries away, shut the house down — and the steps come from the state the house is left in: things carried to other rooms, every plate and glass scattered, lamps and screens on, doors open. The setup does the leaving-wrong; the person is asked only for the goal. Eight tasks across all seven apartments. Each was filmed completing, and the film with the instruction was sent to Gemini 3.1 Pro to ask whether they align clause by clause; the verdict is beside each.
