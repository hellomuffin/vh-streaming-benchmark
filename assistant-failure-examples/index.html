When the assistant is wrong — seven episodes

A simulated person does a household chore in VirtualHome while an AI assistant watches their first-person view and the house map and talks to them. These seven episodes were picked because the person behaves sensibly throughout — few wasted turns, almost no refused actions, natural speech — and the assistant is the one that goes wrong. Between them they show the failures we see most: naming the wrong object, being unable to give directions, answering too late to be useful, being wrong about what the person is holding or has already done, repeating itself, and missing the mistake the benchmark injected. Each clip is cut from that episode’s full film; the transcript below each clip is verbatim from the saved record (“t” = simulation tick, one second; grey rows are the person’s physical actions).
