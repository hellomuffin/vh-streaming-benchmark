When the assistant misses the mistake — five episodes

In each episode a simulated person does a household chore in VirtualHome while an AI assistant watches the first-person view and the house map and talks to them. The benchmark forces one or more realistic mistakes into the person’s actions (taking the wrong object, skipping a step, putting something in the wrong place). The assistant’s job is to notice the mistake and help the person finish. Below are five recent episodes where it did neither: the mistake went unnoticed and the task was not completed. #1–#3 are clear-cut; #4–#5 are weaker (the person caught the mistake themselves). Each clip is cut from the episode’s full film; the transcript under each clip is verbatim from the saved record (“t” = simulation tick, one second).
