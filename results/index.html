The result that nearly came out backwards

Qwen3-VL first scored a flat zero on every detection metric. It was not blind. It emitted 115 of its 118 flags as a bare string — "flag": "wrong_placement" — where the harness accepted only {"error": "wrong_placement"}, so every genuine detection was discarded. Regraded from the models' own replies, it turns out to have the highest precision in the sweep. What it actually fails at is emitting the declared shape, which is now reported as its own metric instead of being silently folded into its detection score.

The headline would otherwise have read "open-weight models cannot detect errors at all". They can; ours could not hear them.

What this measures

The episodes

Each chore carries two or more injected mistakes of different kinds — a forgotten lamp and a repetition, a wrong carton and a stall. The errors are separated in time and cannot be twins of one another, so a flag can be attributed to exactly one of them. The assistant sees the person's camera, a floor plan, and a room-level state map; it can only speak.

The floor

The silent arm never speaks. It is not a strawman: it fixes the interruption cost at its best possible value and shows what the chores do on their own. In-time success is 0.083 without an assistant, so anything above that is help, and anything below it is interference.

Honest limits

  • Three metrics are not measured, and are reported that way rather than as zeros: answer accuracy (these chores ask the assistant no scored questions), alert score (no standing "tell me when it's done" request), and the 15-item assistance-quality rubric (its judge was not run here).
  • Substitutions. No OpenAI key exists in this environment, so the GPT arms could not run at all. The reference spec's gemini-3.7-flash is not offered on this key; gemini-3-flash-preview stands in. The spec's local models (qwen-27b, minicpmo, museglimmer) are not on this filesystem; two open-weight VLMs that are were served instead.
  • One arm's task metrics are not comparable. Because the live harness did not recognise Qwen3-VL's flags, its takeover never released on detection — so its success and efficiency figures understate what it would achieve with a conforming flag. Its detection figures are fair, having been regraded from what it actually said.
  • Success is a hard bar here. Every episode contains a mistake that breaks the outcome, so in-time success can only rise when the assistant actually guides a repair.
