Episode review — the composite suite

Each of the 12 episodes below is one household task performed by a simulated person while an AI assistant watches through a camera and may speak. The engine injects two or three mistakes of different classes into the person's plan; the assistant is scored on noticing each one, naming it with the right label, and helping repair it.

Read each entry as: what the person was asked to do, which errors were injected and where in the plan they were anchored, then a time axis of one real run. On that axis the vertical rule is the tick at which the error went live, the shaded bar is the window in which a flag counts as a detection, and the dot is where the assistant actually caught it.

cx_routine_rep_obj

Having switched the bedroom lamp off she goes back and switches it on again, and later takes the juice out of the fridge instead of the milk.

Task

“TASK: Turn off a bedroom lamp, put a book on the living-room coffee table, heat the milk from the fridge in the microwave, and bring a plate over to the coffee table.”

Clean plan, 8 subgoals: switch_off(tablelamp) → fetch(book) → deposit(book) → fetch(milk) → deposit(milk) → switch_on(microwave) → fetch(plate) → deposit(plate)

Injected errors

#classanchored onwhat it realizes as
1repetitionswitch_off(tablelamp)she goes back to something she has ALREADY FINISHED and does it again. On a switch, a door or any toggle the second press INVERTS what she achieved — the lamp she turned off is lit again, the fridge she shut is open again — so the tell is that she REVISITED a finished target, not that the end state is 'done twice'. On a placement she takes the object out and puts it back.
